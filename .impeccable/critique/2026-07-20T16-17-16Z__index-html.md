## Overall Impression

Four rounds in, this app is in genuinely good shape — the AI-slop and cognitive-load battles are largely won, and the remaining findings are narrower and more specific than before. The one real theme this round: **the step-nav and interaction patterns make promises the app doesn't fully keep**. The step pills imply all 4 steps get the same completion rigor (only Cliente does), and the search-result rows got keyboard accessibility that the four visually-identical client-table rows didn't. Both are "we did this well in one place, forgot to extend it" gaps, not new design problems.

## What's Working

- **The duplicate-RFC guard** distinguishes "already in your list" from "belongs to another vendedor" with copy, not just a blocking error — solves real confusion, not just a technical edge case.
- **The toast system's pause-on-hover/focus + longer duration for actionable toasts** shows real attention to the physical reality of a vendedor mid-conversation on a tablet.
- **`_renderClienteSubnav` generated from the live DOM** rather than hardcoded per tipo — guarantees Física/Moral subnavs never drift out of sync with the fields actually on screen.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Client-table rows are mouse/touch-only; keyboard and screen-reader users can't open a client from any list**
- **Why it matters**: Dashboard recent-clients, "Mis Clientes," "Todos los Clientes," and vendor-detail tables all use `<tr style="cursor:pointer" onclick="navigate(...)">` with no `role="button"`, `tabindex`, or keydown handler — while the dashboard search-result rows got exactly this treatment in a prior round. Same interaction, inconsistent accessibility contract, in 4 places.
- **Fix**: extend the `role="button" tabindex="0" onkeydown="..."` pattern already used on `.search-item` to the table-row templates.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] No required-field enforcement outside the "Cliente" step, despite step pills implying equal rigor across all 4**
- **Why it matters**: `REQUIRED_FIELDS_FISICA`/`REQUIRED_FIELDS_MORAL` only cover Cliente-step fields. VIN, precio, forma de pago, fecha/lugar de entrega are optional in code even though they populate legally-facing sale documents. A vendedor can save a client and print a compraventa with a blank price or VIN with zero warning.
- **Fix**: mark VIN and Precio (fields that appear on every generated document) as required with the same missing-field toast pattern, and/or add a lightweight completion indicator to the Vehículo/Finanzas/Entrega pills mirroring the Cliente one.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Printed documents disappearing from the list is a one-way door disguised as reversible**
- **Why it matters**: Once printed, a document hides and only reappears by re-saving the entire client record — which also resets `printed_docs`/`hidden_docs` for every document, not just the one that needs reprinting. A vendedor whose printer jammed on one document has no proportionate way to just reprint that one thing.
- **Fix**: add a small "Ver documentos ya impresos" toggle on the doc-grid view listing hidden docs with an explicit reimprimir action, independent of editing client data.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P3] `entregaObservaciones` free-text field has no length guard or character counter**
- **Why it matters**: It's the only unconstrained free-text field in an otherwise tightly select-driven form; a vendedor has no feedback on how a long note will land in the generated document until previewing it. Low risk, minor polish.
- **Fix**: cap the textarea with a small counter, or note in the placeholder that it prints as-is.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

## Persona Red Flags

**Vendedor (tablet, sales floor, mid-conversation)**: the silent-success-with-blank-vehicle-data issue is a direct hit — this persona fills fast under social pressure and is most likely to skip VIN or precio, with zero warning until the printed compraventa is already in the customer's hands.

**Gerente/Administrador (reviewing multiple vendedores)**: sortable columns exist on "Mis Clientes"/"Todos los Clientes" but not on the Vendor Clients detail table or Activity Log — a gerente cross-referencing one vendedor's list loses the sort capability they just got used to one screen over. Minor, not urgent.

**New vendedor (first week, unfamiliar with fiscal jargon)**: FSOS, BSI Plus, AMDA, A1/A2 remain raw acronyms with no inline definition — known and explicitly out of scope this round, but still the single biggest onboarding-friction point left in the app.

## Minor Observations

- The disabled Persona Física/Moral tabs correctly use `title=` to explain *why* they're disabled — worth reusing this pattern for any other disabled controls in the app.
- `_focusStepField` has a genuinely well-reasoned inline comment about why it must switch steps before `.focus()` — rare and valuable documentation for a single-file app this size.
- New duplicate id `edit-user-avatar-preview` (3 occurrences) follows the established safe mutually-exclusive-render pattern — no action needed, flagged for awareness only.
- `.doc-template` print/PDF block's hardcoded hex colors are a separate concern from the themed UI (it's a print stylesheet, not a screen surface) — not counted against the token-system finding from before.

## Questions to Consider

- If the whole point of this app is generating legally-facing documents, why is the document engine less strict about required data than the client intake form — shouldn't "can I legally print this" gate document generation rather than relying on the vendedor to remember to fill Vehículo fields nobody enforced?
- The search-item rows got full keyboard accessibility and the four table-row equivalents didn't — was that a deliberate scoping decision in a prior round, or did it just not get re-checked once the pattern existed elsewhere?
- Is "printed documents vanish until you re-save the whole client" the desired product behavior, or an implementation shortcut that happened to feel reasonable in isolation?
